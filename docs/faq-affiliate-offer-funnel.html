Simple funnel to promote one affiliate offer

Page → optional email capture → clear CTA to one offer. Affairs itself uses affiliate links (disclosed above); this page is research framing for your promo path — no earnings claims, no “what we made.”

Minimum affiliate promo shape

  1. Honest landing. Who the offer is for, who it is not for, and that you may earn a commission.
  2. Optional list capture. If people are not ready to click today — welcome + educational follow-up (see lead magnet).
  3. One primary CTA. Deep link with your affiliate parameters; avoid five competing buttons.
  4. Disclosure. FTC-style note near the recommendation, not buried in a footer only.

How Affairs handles this (example, not a blueprint for income)

  • Live systeme.io CTAs use an affiliate href with rel="sponsored" and data-affiliate; Kit / Leadpages / Surfer stay placeholders.
