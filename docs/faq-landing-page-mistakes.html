Common first-landing page mistakes (checklist)

A research-style checklist for first landing pages — clarity, one CTA, honest proof, and not buying tools before the message is clear. Not fake case studies or conversion guarantees.

Checklist (research frame)

  1. Unclear offer. Visitor cannot say in one sentence what they get and for whom.
  2. Multiple CTAs. Competing buttons (buy / book / subscribe / social) dilute the next step.
  3. Proof theater. Fake testimonials, invented star ratings, or “as seen in” without substance — we avoid those on Affairs too.
  4. Form before value. Long gated forms with no sample of the lead magnet (see lead magnet FAQ).
  5. Tool-first rebuild. Migrating builders before the headline and offer are stable (see landing builder and switching).
  6. No mobile pass. Unreadable type, tiny tap targets, or a checkout that breaks on phone.
  7. Missing disclosure. Affiliate or sponsored recommendations without an above-fold note.

Fix order (before buying another tool)

  • Rewrite headline + subhead for one audience and one outcome.
  • Pick one primary CTA; move secondary links below the fold.
  • Replace vague claims with specifics you can stand behind (features, scope, refund policy) — still no fabricated social proof.
  • Then decide DIY vs builder vs AIO — see checkout vs funnel and vs DIY.
