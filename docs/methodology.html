Methodology

How this free GitHub Pages MVP ranks three named tools — and what it does not claim.

Scope

Cycle-1 always returns the same three slots: Kit (email for creators), Leadpages (landing pages), and Surfer (SEO / AI content visibility). The quiz reorders those three. It does not search a wider catalog.

Quiz weights

Each tool starts at score 0. We add points from your three answers, then sort high → low. Ties keep the default order Kit → Leadpages → Surfer.

Budget (only where a vendor publishes a clear signal)

  • Free / freemium: Kit +2 (Kit publishes a free plan). Leadpages 0 (site lists paid plans from $99/mo). Surfer 0 (no free-plan claim cited here).
  • Starter (~$20–50/mo): Kit +2. Leadpages 0 (published starting price sits above this band). Surfer 0 (price not cited on this page).
  • Team (~$50–200/mo): Leadpages +2 (published $99/mo Grow plan falls in-band). Kit +1, Surfer +1.
  • Enterprise: Surfer +2 (markets to agencies and enterprises). Leadpages +1. Kit 0 (creator / solo positioning).

Team size

  • Solo: Kit +2 (creator / solo-business positioning). Others +1.
  • Small (2–10): Kit +2, Leadpages +2, Surfer +1.
  • Mid (11–50) / Large (50+): Surfer +2 and Leadpages +2 (agencies / content / campaign teams). Kit +1.

Research vs hands-on

Card copy paraphrases what each vendor says about itself on official pages (kit.com, leadpages.com, surferseo.com, checked 11 Sep 2026).

  • Labels stay: “Research placeholder — not a hands-on review.”
  • We do not invent testimonials, star ratings, or “we tested this.”
  • Fit notes are category-level (email for creators / landing pages / SEO content), not performance claims.
  • Vendor marketing can change; re-check their sites before you buy.
