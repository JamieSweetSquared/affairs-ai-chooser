Simple course / digital-product funnel (no agency)

Page → email → checkout for a solo course or digital-product launch — without an agency retainer or a 12-page funnel. Research-labeled options; not a hands-on review or earnings claim.

Minimum course funnel shape

  1. Offer page. Who it’s for, what’s inside, price, one checkout CTA.
  2. Optional lead magnet / waitlist. Capture interested people who are not ready to buy today.
  3. Email path. Welcome + a short lesson or proof sequence; soft CTA back to the offer.
  4. Checkout + delivery. Payment, then access (course portal, PDF, or community link).

Skip upsell trees and webinar stacks until the core offer converts with people you already know.

When DIY / separate wins

  • DIY: Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / Stripe Payment Link + a static page + a free ESP can sell a PDF or cohort before you need a funnel builder.
  • Specialist course host: you already have a list and only need LMS features the AIO does not emphasize.
  • Creator ESP first: audience building is the whole job — see creator email FAQ.
  • Landing CRO first: paid traffic + A/B pages — see landing builder FAQ.
