How recommendations work

  1. Primary need is the main weight. SEO / writing boosts Surfer; creator email / list work boosts Kit; campaign pages boost Leadpages; funnel + email on a budget boosts systeme.io.
  2. Budget and team size are modifiers, only where a vendor publishes a clear fit (e.g. Kit’s and systeme.io’s free plans; Leadpages’ paid landing-page plans starting at $99/mo on their site).
  3. Support / chat and scheduling / ops do not map to these products as dedicated jobs. We still show the cycle-1 slots, reordered by nearest published-fit, with a mismatch note.
