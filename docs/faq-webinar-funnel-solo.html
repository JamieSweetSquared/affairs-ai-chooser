Simple webinar / workshop funnel for solos

Registration → reminder email → live (or replay) session → offer — without a 20-page “webinar machine.” Research-labeled fit framing; not a hands-on review, star rating, or earnings claim.

Minimum webinar / workshop shape

  1. Registration page. Topic, date/time (or “on-demand”), who it’s for, one form CTA.
  2. Confirmation + reminders. Email (and optional SMS later) so people actually show up.
  3. Session. Live Zoom/Meet or a recorded workshop; keep the offer clear and late enough to teach first.
  4. Offer path. Soft CTA during/after; checkout or booking page; short follow-up sequence for no-shows and replays.

Skip evergreen 12-email “webinar funnels” until one live workshop converts with people you already know.

When something else fits better

  • Zoom + Google Form + free ESP: fine for a first workshop with an existing audience.
  • Specialist webinar platforms: if you need advanced room features the AIO does not emphasize.
  • Course without a live session: see course funnel instead.
  • Lead magnet only: see lead magnet → email.
