What to buy first on ≤$50–100/mo?

Decision guide across the cycle-1 slots (Kit, Leadpages, Surfer, systeme.io) for a tight monthly budget. Honest about when to skip. Research-labeled from published positioning — not hands-on reviews.

Suggested buy order (constraint-first)

Pricing signals cited from vendor sites as of 11 Sep 2026. Re-check before you pay. This is a fit / budget frame, not a ranked product review.

  1. Own the audience (Kit slot) first if email is the job. Kit publishes a free plan with subscriber limits — often the only cycle-1 tool that fits a free / starter band without stretching. See creator email FAQ.
  2. DIY the launch page before paying for Leadpages. Leadpages’ public Grow plan is listed from $99/mo — that alone can consume a ≤$100 stack. Prefer ESP forms / a static page until campaign CRO is clearly the bottleneck. See landing page FAQ.
  3. Consider systeme.io when you want funnel + email in one bill. It publishes a permanently free plan (contact / funnel caps) and a low paid band (Startup cited ~$17/mo). Often a substitute for stacking a creator ESP and a landing-page builder — not a specialist SEO editor. See funnel + email FAQ.
  4. Add Surfer only if you ship SEO content on a cadence. Solo writers who publish rarely should skip paid SEO editors until search / AI-answer traffic is a real channel. See SEO solo FAQ.

When to buy nothing yet

  • No audience, no content cadence, and no paid/email traffic to a page — tooling will not invent demand.
  • Your host / ESP / CMS already covers the next milestone.
  • You are comparing tools instead of shipping the launch.
