Simple email automations for creators (no agency)

The smallest automation set that still helps a solo creator — without an agency retainer or a 40-step flowchart. Research-labeled options; not a hands-on review.

Minimum automation set

  1. Welcome / deliverability. Instant delivery of the lead magnet + who you are.
  2. Short nurture. 3–5 useful emails teaching one idea; soft CTA to the offer.
  3. Offer follow-up. One reminder path after someone visits the sales page or abandons checkout (if your tool supports it).
  4. Tag or segment lightly. “Has offer” vs “nurture only” is enough at the start.

Skip branching trees until you have reply data. Complexity is not a growth strategy.

When not to automate yet

  • Fewer than a handful of subscribers and no weekly publishing habit.
  • You have not written a manual welcome you would be proud to send.
  • You need transactional product email or a helpdesk — different jobs; not these cycle-1 slots.
