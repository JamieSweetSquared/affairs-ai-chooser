Digital product funnel planner — first vs second product

Map the minimum path: offer → landing → checkout → delivery → follow-up. Toggle first vs second product; note tools you already own before buying anything. Research-labeled — not a revenue playbook, earnings claim, or hands-on review.

Which product are you planning?

Product context
First product: Prefer the shortest path that can take money and deliver access. Clarity of offer beats template count. See also launch checklist and first funnel for solos.
Second product: Reuse landing patterns, email tags, and checkout wiring from product one. Ask whether you need another platform — or another page + delivery path inside tools you already pay for. Related: course / digital-product funnel.

Minimum steps (required capabilities)

  1. Offer. One sentence a stranger understands: who it is for, what they get, price.
  2. Landing. One primary CTA (buy or waitlist) — see landing mistakes.
  3. Checkout. Payment link or checkout page; test once yourself.
  4. Delivery. Course area, file link, or email with access — immediately after purchase.
  5. Follow-up. Welcome / receipt + one nurture or support path (not a 40-step flowchart).

Optional upgrades (webinars, A/B tests, affiliate portals, communities) come later. Do not treat optional features as launch blockers — see when NOT to buy funnel software.
