Decision question

Do you need membership on WordPress you already own (levels, protected posts/pages, drip, WP-native checkout), or a hosted all-in-one that ships page + email + course + checkout without plugins?

Who the MemberPress / WordPress path fits

A WP + MemberPress path is a strong candidate when most of these are true:

  • The site of record is already WordPress and you want memberships / paywalled content on that site — not a second hosted course platform.
  • The job is access control + membership billing + drip, not a hosted page/funnel/email builder (email may still be a separate tool — see FluentCRM vs AIO for WP email-CRM adjacency).
  • You prefer self-hosted / own-the-data and will accept plugin updates, hosting, and payment gateway setup.
  • You may already use Woo, an LMS, or other WP plugins and want memberships next to that stack.
  • You are not starting from zero tools with no site: you already have WordPress and a reason to stay there.

When an all-in-one (systeme.io) is simpler

A hosted funnel + email + course all-in-one is usually the simpler first buy when WordPress is not the constraint. Related: funnel + email AIO · AIO vs stack · is systeme’s free plan enough? · course / digital-product funnel.

  • You do not have a WordPress site (or do not want to maintain plugins, themes, backups, and gateways).
  • The bottleneck is page + email + checkout or a simple course in one login — MemberPress does not replace a hosted funnel + ESP by itself.
  • You are validating one offer and would rather ship a path than assemble a WP membership stack.
  • You need email broadcasts/sequences without adding a separate ESP or WP email plugin.
  • You already ran (or will run) the chooser and the funnel + email job surfaces the systeme.io slot.

When not to buy (yet)

  • No offer, no list, and no WordPress site — tooling will not invent demand (start with the chooser or a hosted free/starter path).
  • You only need a one-time checkout link — a full membership plugin or funnel suite may be overkill (see checkout vs funnel).
  • You already pay for a WP membership + ESP + page builder stack that works — consolidating “for fun” is not a reason (see renewal decision).
  • You expected recurring affiliate % beyond published “first sale” terms — Affairs will not invent that claim.
