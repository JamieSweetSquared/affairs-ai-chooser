Pick the job, then the tool shape

On ≤~$100/mo, do not stack a specialist ESP + paid landing builder + SEO editor + AIO. Ship one job this week.

Research-labeled fit signals — confirm live pricing on vendor sites
If your job is… Lean toward… Skip / defer when…
Newsletter / list / creator email Email-first tools (Kit-shaped slot). Free plans often exist for starters. You mainly need pages + checkout in one login — an AIO may replace a separate ESP.
Campaign landing / CRO pages only Landing-page builders (Leadpages-shaped). DIY (Carrd/static) until CRO is the bottleneck. Published Grow-class pricing (~$99/mo) alone can eat a ≤$100 band — DIY first.
Pages + email + simple checkout / funnel All-in-one (systeme.io publishes free/starter + low paid bands). You already love a specialist ESP and only need a form embed — keep DIY pages.
SEO / AI-visibility writing cadence Surfer-shaped SEO editors — only if you publish on a real cadence. Rare posts or no search channel yet — skip paid SEO editors.

Quick rules of thumb

  • One login for funnel + email on a budget? Start with systeme.io’s published free plan — see is the free plan enough?.
  • Email is the product? Prefer a creator ESP path before paying for a landing builder.
  • Unsure on monthly spend? Use the solo stack cost calculator (illustrative bands — not quotes).
