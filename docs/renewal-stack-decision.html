Your stack is renewing — keep it, or consolidate under $50–$100/mo?

A decision worksheet for when email, landing, and funnel tools hit renewal. Enter your own renewal amounts (preferred) or use illustrative research bands. This is fit framing — not a savings promise, earnings claim, or hands-on review.

Why renewals force the question

Separate tools for email, pages, and checkout can each look “cheap” until they renew in the same month. On a ≤$50–$100 ceiling, stacking a creator ESP + landing builder + SEO editor usually overshoots — see what to buy first on ≤$100/mo. Consolidation only makes sense if required features still fit one login (or you keep what already works).
