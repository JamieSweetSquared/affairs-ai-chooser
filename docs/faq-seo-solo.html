When NOT to buy

  • You publish rarely (a few posts a year) — free SERP reading + an outline may be enough.
  • You have not validated that search / AI-answer traffic is a real channel for your topic yet.
  • Your budget should go to email list ownership or a single launch page first (see stack guide).
  • You already subscribe to another SEO suite that covers on-page guidance you actually use.
